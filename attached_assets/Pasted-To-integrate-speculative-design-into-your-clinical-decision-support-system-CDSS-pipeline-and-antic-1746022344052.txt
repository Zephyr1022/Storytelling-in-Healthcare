To integrate speculative design into your clinical decision support system (CDSS) pipeline and anticipate potential ethical harms, you can develop narrative scenarios that highlight how biases might affect patient outcomes based on attributes like gender, age, and race. These stories can serve as powerful tools in fairness training, making abstract concepts more tangible and fostering empathy among healthcare professionals.

⸻

🧩 Integrating Speculative Design into the CDSS Pipeline

Building upon the refined CDSS pipeline, speculative design can be interwoven at each stage to identify and address potential biases:
	1.	Initial Patient-Provider Interaction
	•	Speculative Scenario: A clinician, influenced by unconscious biases, underestimates the severity of symptoms reported by a young Black woman, leading to delayed diagnosis.
	2.	Data Entry into Electronic Health Record (EHR)
	•	Speculative Scenario: Incomplete or biased data entry for elderly patients results in the AI model lacking critical information, affecting recommendation accuracy.
	3.	AI-Powered Clinical Decision Support System
	•	Speculative Scenario: The AI model, trained predominantly on data from a specific demographic, provides less accurate recommendations for patients from underrepresented groups.
	4.	Generation of Recommendations
	•	Speculative Scenario: The system suggests a treatment plan that doesn’t consider cultural or socioeconomic factors, potentially leading to non-adherence.
	5.	Clinician Evaluation and Decision-Making
	•	Speculative Scenario: A clinician over-relies on AI recommendations without critically assessing their applicability to the patient’s unique context.
	6.	Follow-Up Patient-Provider Interaction
	•	Speculative Scenario: The patient feels their concerns are dismissed due to a lack of personalized care, eroding trust in the healthcare system.

🎭 Crafting Speculative Narratives for Fairness Training

Developing detailed stories based on these scenarios can enhance fairness training: ￼
	•	Case Study: “The Overlooked Pain”
	•	Profile: A 45-year-old Hispanic male with chronic back pain.
	•	Scenario: The AI model, trained on data lacking representation from Hispanic populations, underestimates the severity of his condition, leading to inadequate pain management.
	•	Training Focus: Highlighting the importance of diverse training data and clinician vigilance in interpreting AI recommendations.
	•	Case Study: “Silent Symptoms”
	•	Profile: An 80-year-old Asian female presenting with atypical symptoms of a heart attack.
	•	Scenario: The AI system fails to flag her condition due to age and gender biases in symptom presentation data.
	•	Training Focus: Emphasizing the need for clinicians to consider atypical presentations and question AI outputs.

⸻

🛠️ Implementing Speculative Design in Training Programs

To effectively incorporate these narratives:
	•	Interactive Workshops: Engage healthcare professionals in role-playing exercises based on the scenarios to foster empathy and critical thinking.
	•	Storytelling Sessions: Use multimedia presentations to convey patient experiences, followed by group discussions on potential biases and mitigation strategies. ￼
	•	Feedback Mechanisms: Encourage participants to share their insights and experiences, enriching the training material with real-world perspectives.
